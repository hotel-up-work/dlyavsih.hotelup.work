## About
"Для всіх" is a small complex in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine, located at провулок Північний, 3. It combines short-term lodging with a car wash and other services, positioned as a stop for both travelers and drivers: "Для вашої поїздки. Для вашого авто." The page offers three room categories (Двомісний стандарт, Напівлюкс, Люкс) alongside car washing for cars and trucks, a kitchen, and a sauna with a gazebo for outdoor relaxation. Several details (room counts, capacity, pricing, hours) are explicitly marked on the page as not yet confirmed.
